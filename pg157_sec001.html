4.
Identify similar triangles
  • (a) (Triangle 1): △ABP (object triangle) and △A1B1P (image triangle) are similar
    • (i) ∠APB=∠A1PB1 (from angle of incidence = angle of reflection).
    • (ii) Both are right-angled.
  • (b) (Triangle 2): △DEF (Mirror-focal triangle) and △A1B1F (Image-focal triangle) are similar
    • (i) ∠EFD=∠B1FA1 (Vertically opposite angles)
    • (ii) Both are right-angled.
5.
Set up a ratio
From △ABP:△A1B1P
A1B1AB=B1PBP⇒hih0=vu(1)
△DEF:△A1B1F
A1B1DE=B1FFE⇒hih0=v−ff(2)
6.
Compare Equations (1) and (2) :
vu=v−ff
vf=uv-uf
vfuv=uvuv−ufuv
fu=1−fv
f(1v+1u)=1
1v+1u=1f

This equation applies to both concave and convex mirrors with both convention signs.
