7.

For each concave mirror, you experiment with image formation for three values of object distances: 10 cm, 20 cm, and 30 cm.

(a) For the three object distances, identify which mirror produces an image with a magnification of -1.

(b) Out of the three mirrors, identify which one you would prefer to use for saving purposes or for applying makeup.

(c) For mirror B, create a ray diagram to illustrate image formation for object distances of 10 cm and 20 cm.
