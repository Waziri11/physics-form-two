Properties of magnets

When you closely examine a bar magnet, you see one end marked N and the other end marked S. Every magnet, regardless of its shape and size, has two poles. N represents a North pole and S represents a South pole as shown in Figure 3.6.

Figure 3.6: Bar magnet showing the N and S poles

These poles are always marked during the process of manufacturing for easy identification. Colouring is also used to mark the poles as shown in Figure 3.6.

Aim:To investigate the properties of magnets

Procedure

1.

Perform the following steps:

(a) Position the north pole of one bar magnet close to the south pole of another bar magnet.

(b) Bring the two north poles close, then do the same with the two south poles.

(c) Make a note of what you observe.

(d) Spread some iron filings onto a piece of paper, then hold the north pole of a magnet near the filings without touching them.

(e) Repeat this by bringing the south pole close to the filings, again without contact.

(f) Lastly, bring the centre part of the magnet near the filings, but do not let them touch.

(g) Record your observations.
