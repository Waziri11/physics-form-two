Chapter summary

  • 1.Optical instruments use refraction or reflection of light to form different types of images.
  • 2.Microscopes make it possible to see very small objects by producing an enlarged image of an object.
  • 3.Telescopes and binoculars allow us to see objects that are far away.
  • 4.Cameras provide us with a recorded image of an object. The image can be a still image or a moving image (video).
  • 5.The human eye can discern colour, judge distance and see great detail from both near and far positions by adjusting the focal length of its lens.
  • 6.Myopia is a condition in which close objects appear clearly, but far ones
