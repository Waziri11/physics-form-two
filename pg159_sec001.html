Therefore, the image is formed at a distance of 18.28 cm from the mirror with a size of 8.81 cm.The negative sign of the image distance indicates that the image is formed behind the mirror.

Example 4.6

A 4.0 cm light bulb is placed 35.5 cm from a convex mirror with a focal length of 12.2 cm.Determine the image distance and the image size.

Solution

By the new Cartesian sign conversion;

u=-35.5\ cm  (Measured against the direction of incident light),
f=+12.2 cm

From,

1f=1u+1v
1v=1f−1u
v=fuu−f
v=−35.5 cm×12.2 cm−35.5 cm−12.2 cm=9.08 cm
m=−vu=−9.08 cm−35.5 cm=0.26
hi=mho=0.26×4 cm=1.04 cm

Therefore, the image size is 1.04 cm, and the image distance is 9.08 cm behind the mirror.
