Differences between additive and subtractive colour mixing

The easy way to remember the difference between additive and subtractive colour mixing is that, additive colour mixing is what happens when we mix lights of different colours whereas subtractive colour mixing occurs when we mix paints or other coloured material.

Table 5.4 summarizes the differences between the two-colour mixing processes.

Table 5.4: Comparison between additive and subtractive colour mixing
