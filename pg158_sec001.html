Generally, the sign conventions for the given quantities in the mirror equation and magnification equations are as follows;

  • (a) f is positive if the mirror is concave.
  • (b) f is negative if the mirror is convex.
  • (c) v is positive if the image is real and located on the same side of the mirror as the object.
  • (d) v is negative if the image is virtual and located behind the mirror.
  • (e) hi is positive if the image is upright and, therefore, virtual.
  • (f) hi is negative if the image is inverted and, therefore, real.

Example 4.5

A 4.0 cm light bulb is placed 8.3 cm from a concave mirror with a focal length of 15.2 cm. Determine the image distance and the image size.

Solution

Given h0=4.0 cm ,u=8.3 cm,
f=15.2 cm

Using formula

1f=1u+1v

Upon rearranging the formula

v=fuu−f
v=15.2 cm×8.3 cm8.3 cm−15.2 cm=−18.28 cm

From m=hih0=−vu, hence, hi=−vh0u

hi=−(−18.28 cm)×4 cm8.3 cm=8.81 cm
