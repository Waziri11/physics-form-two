Example 4.2

Two plane mirrors are placed at an angle of 60° to each other. When an object is placed between them, find the number of images formed due to repeated reflections.

Solution

Given θ=60∘

Substitute into the formula

n=360∘θ−1
n=360∘θ−1=n=360∘60∘−1
n = 6 - 1 = 5

Therefore, 5 images are formed due to repeated reflections.
