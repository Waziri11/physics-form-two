4. Like poles repel and unlike poles attract.

5. The ‘tiny magnets’ in a material are called dipoles. A group of dipoles forms a domain.

7. Points at which the net magnetic field is zero are called neutral points.

8. Materials that can redirect field lines are called permeable materials.

9. The angle between the resultant magnetic field direction and horizontal component of the field is called the angle of inclination, I, or dip angle.
