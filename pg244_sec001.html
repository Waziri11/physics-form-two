7. Hyperopia is a condition in which images of nearby objects are blurry, whereby the image is formed behind the retina.

8. Presbyopia is the gradual loss of the eye’s ability to focus on nearby objects, and it occurs because of old age.

9. Astigmatism is the imperfection in the curvature of the eye that causes the formation of blurred images.

It occurs when either the cornea or the lens has mismatched curves.
