Real-is-positive convention

To determine the values of u and v using the formula, a sign convention is adopted. The convention is referred to as the real-is-positive convention. When using this convention, all distances are measured from the optical centre. Distances of real objects and real images are treated as positive whereas distances of virtual objects and images are taken to be negative. Because the principal focus of a concave lens is virtual, concave lenses have negative values of focal length.
