Activity 2.12

Aim: To observe the effect of resistance on the heating effect of an electric current

Materials: Battery, calorimeter with jacket, thermometer, resistive wire, ammeter, voltmeter, rheostat, stopwatch, water, switch, stirrer, connecting wires

Procedure

1. Set up an arrangement as shown in Figure 2.37.

2. Close switch S and adjust a rheostat to obtain suitable values of I and V. Record the initial temperature of water.

3. Leave the current ON for a known time and record the change in temperature.
4. Change the resistance of the wire by varying its length, then, repeat steps 2 and 3.
5. Repeat step 4 and obtain at least 2 more readings of change of temperature with the corresponding resistance of wire.
6. Determine the resistance of each length of the wire and record the results as shown in Table 2.10.
