Potential difference across a conductor, current and resistance

Performing Activity 2.4 can determine the relationship between p.d across a conductor, the current flowing through the conductor, and the conductor’s resistance and performing activity 2.5 one can determine the unknown resistance in circuit by ohms law.

Activity 2.4

Aim:

To determine the relationship between potential difference, current and resistance

Materials:

a d.c. power supply or 1.5 V cell, ammeter, bulb, switch, voltmeter, rheostat, connecting wires

Procedure

  1. 1. Connect an electric circuit as shown in Figure 2.25.

  2. 2. With the switch open, measure and record the potential difference across the bulb and the current through the circuit by using the voltmeter and ammeter, respectively.

  3. 3. Close the switch and adjust the rheostat until the voltmeter reads 0.2 V.

  4. 4. Measure and record the current through the bulb. Observe the brightness of the bulb.

  5. 5. Continue increasing the p.d at intervals of 0.2 V up to 1.2 V by adjusting the rheostat. Read the corresponding current value in each case.
