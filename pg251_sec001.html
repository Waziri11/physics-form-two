Glossary

Dip needle

is an instrument used to measure the angle of inclination of a magnetic field.

Demagnetisation

is the process of destroying the magnetic property or magnetism of an object.

Electrostatic

is the study of stationary electric charges.

Electrophorus

is a simple manual electric generator used to produce electrostatic charges by electrostatic induction.

Electrostatic force

is the push or pull of electric charges at rest.

Electric circuit

a closed path for electric charge to flow through. It consists of a voltage source, connecting wires and devices such as resistors and switches.

Electrical conductor

material that permits current to flow freely through it under an applied voltage. Metals are good conductors.

Electric current

is the rate of flow of electrons, or the sustained movement of electrical charge.

Electrification

is the process of introducing electric charges to a body so that it is electrically charged.

Electromagnet

is a type of magnet whose magnetic property is produced by an electric current.

Focal plane

is an area in a camera where light is focused.

Farad

is the SI unit for capacitance.

Ferromagnetic

magnetic materials that can be permanently magnetised.

Magnification

is the apparent enlargement of an object by an optical instrument.

Magnetic poles

are the two ends of a magnet or magnetised body where the lines of force are most concentrated. The strength of a magnet is strongest at the poles.

Magnetic shielding

process of limiting the passage of magnetic field lines through a region by diverging them.

Magnetisation

involves aligning the dipoles in a material to produce a net effect of attraction or repulsion. It is the process of making magnets.

Magnetism

is a physical phenomenon arising from the force caused by magnets.
