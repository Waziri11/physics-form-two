Solution

(a) Energy = power × time

=40 W×5 hdays×30 days
=6 000 Wh=6 kWh

Therefore, the electrical energy consumed for 30 days is 6 kWh.

(b) To determine the electricity cost of watching this TV, we use the formula;

cost (Tsh)=price×total units

Therefore,

cost (Tsh)=229.60 Tsh1 kWh×6 kWh
=1 377.60 Tsh

Hence, the cost of watching this TV for 30 days is Tanzanian shillings 1377.60.
