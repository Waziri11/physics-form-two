Thus, c=η1v1=η2v2

Hence, η1v1=η2v2

This expression can be rearranged to read:

η2η1=v1v2

The ratio η2η1 is the relative refractive index of glass to water. This ratio can also be related to the ratio of the sines of the angle of incidence and the angle of refraction as follows.

η2η1=sin⁡(i)sin⁡(r)

Therefore, η1sin⁡(i)=η2sin⁡(r)

Applying the law of reversibility of light shows that, gηa=1aηg where gηa and aηg are the relative refractive indices for light travelling from air to glass and from glass to air, respectively.
