Sources of light

Light sources can be classified into two main types: natural and artificial.

Some objects are luminous, meaning they produce their own light, whereas others are non-luminous and only reflect or scatter light from other sources.

Bioluminescence is a natural occurrence in certain glowing organisms, especially in dark forest settings.
