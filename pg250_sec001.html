Glossary

Accommodation (of the eye)

is the adjustment of the focusing power of the eye to see objects clearly over a range of distances. Accommodation is achieved by changing the shape of the crystalline lens.

Angle of deviation

is the angle through which the direction of a ray of light is changed by a refracting surface of a prism. It is the angle between the incident and refracted (or emergent) ray.

Apical angle

is an angle included between refracting surfaces and which is contained in a principal section of a prism.

Ammeter

a device for measuring electric current in a circuit. It is calibrated in amperes (A).

Alnico

is a family of iron alloys that, in addition to iron, are composed primarily of aluminium, nickel, or cobalt.

Ampere

is the SI unit of electric current and indicates the rate at which charge flows in a circuit.

Battery

is several electric cells connected to produce electric current through converting chemical energy to electrical energy.

Critical angle

is the angle of incidence in the denser medium for which the angle of refraction in the less dense medium is 90°. It is the smallest angle of incidence at which total internal reflection occurs.

Conduction electrons

free electrons that can move from one atom to another within a material.

Contact charging

is the process of transferring electric charges from one body to another by making the bodies touch each other or by rubbing.

Coulomb

is a quantity of electric charge that passes through a given point in a circuit in one second when a steady current of 1 ampere flows. It is the SI unit of electric charge.

Dispersion (of white light)

is the splitting of visible white light into its component colours by a dispersive medium. This occurs because each colour has its own frequency of dispersion.
