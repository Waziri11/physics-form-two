6. A convex mirror is a mirror that curves outward, producing smaller images and a wider field of view.

7. A virtual image is formed by the apparent divergence of light rays, which cannot be projected onto a screen (e.g., images in a plane mirror).

8. Real image is an image formed by the actual convergence of light rays, which can be projected onto a screen.

9. Focal point is the point at which parallel light rays converge after reflecting off a concave mirror.

10. Magnification (m) is the ratio of the height of the image to the height of the object, indicating how much larger or smaller the image is compared to the object.
